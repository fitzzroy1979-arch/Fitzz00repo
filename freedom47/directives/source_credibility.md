# Directive: Source credibility rules (shared)

Applied by every directive that pulls analyst opinion. Not a runnable directive — a rulebook.

## Analyst credibility bar (all must hold)
1. Established track record or affiliation (named publication, verified TradingView profile with history, channel with a body of prior work)
2. Shows reasoning — a view with a "why", not just a price target
3. No pump/hype markers (guaranteed returns, "100x", urgency language, undisclosed positions in micro-caps)
4. Independent of referral-link monetization for the instrument being discussed

## Approved source classes
- TradingView top analysts (ideas with visible reputation)
- FX analysis sites: FXStreet, DailyFX, ForexLive, Forex Factory
- Credible blogs meeting the bar above
- YouTube — **metadata only** (title, description, upload date). Never video/audio content. Regular uploads, Shorts, and ended/VOD'd streams are in scope; currently-live streams are excluded (not indexed until they end).
- Priority YouTube channels checked every run: `@AlexanderELorenzo`, `@24hrsCrypto`
- Investopedia — reference/definitional context only. Never treated as a trade call.

## Fact-check rule
Every factual claim attributed to an analyst is checked against at least one independent source before inclusion. Unverified claims are **kept and flagged**, not dropped:
- `confirmed` — independent source agrees
- `uncertain` — no independent source found
- `contradicted` — independent source disagrees (show both)

## Crypto-only context
For crypto symbols, include Bitcoin halving-cycle context (historical halving dates; days/months since the most recent) with the standing caveat: four data points from a structurally different market, not a predictive rule.
